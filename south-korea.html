MY DREAM COUNTRY

South Korea 대한민국

Why South Korea?

South Korea is a country that perfectly blends deep historical roots with vibrant modern pop culture, and it completely fascinates me. I dream of exploring the energetic, bustling streets of Seoul, trying mouth-watering authentic Korean street foods like tteokbokki and Korean BBQ, and wandering through the grand historical palaces like Gyeongbokgung. I want to see how the old world and the new world coexist so beautifully in this country.

I have always admired their massive influence on music, fashion, and entertainment, which adds a unique, colorful energy to the country. I want to feel that lively vibe firsthand, see the stunning cherry blossoms in full bloom during spring, and maybe even escape to the breathtaking natural beauty of Jeju Island. For me, South Korea is a place full of life, flavor, and endless things to discover.
