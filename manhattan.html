Manhattan New York

Why Manhattan?

Manhattan is the ultimate symbol of ambition, dreams, and pure energy. I have always been drawn to the idea of standing in the middle of Times Square, completely surrounded by the blinding bright lights and massive screens, feeling the pulse of the city that never sleeps. I want to take a peaceful horse-drawn carriage or a simple walk through Central Park, and look at the iconic, towering skyline from the top of the Empire State Building at night.

There is something incredibly magical and inspiring about the fast-paced life there—the iconic yellow cabs rushing by, the melting pot of diverse people from all over the world, and the endless opportunities that seem to wait around every corner. It is a city that makes you feel like anything is possible if you just chase it. Experiencing that relentless, exciting energy is something I really look forward to someday.
